SPEEDEX LOGO PLACEHOLDER

To add your SpeedEX logo:

1. Save your SpeedEX logo image file as "speedex-logo.png" in this img/ directory
2. Recommended logo specifications:
   - Format: PNG (with transparency) or JPG
   - Size: 200-300px width, maintain aspect ratio
   - Background: Transparent or white background
   - Resolution: High quality (300 DPI recommended)

3. Once you add the logo file, uncomment the logo image line in the HTML files:
   - In index.html (and other HTML files), find this line:
     <!-- <img src="img/speedex-logo.png" alt="SpeedEX Logo"> -->
   - Remove the <!-- --> comment markers to display the logo

4. You may also want to adjust the CSS styling for the logo in css/style.css if needed

The current setup uses a text-based logo with a gas pump icon, but you can replace this with your actual logo image.
