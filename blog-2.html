EN590 Diesel Standards: Ensuring Quality & Performance

10 January 2025

Quality Standards

EN590 diesel standards represent the gold standard in fuel quality, ensuring optimal engine performance, reduced emissions, and enhanced reliability. At SpeedEX, we maintain these rigorous specifications to deliver premium diesel fuel solutions.

What is EN590 Standard?

EN590 is a European standard that defines the quality requirements for diesel fuel used in automotive applications. This comprehensive specification ensures that diesel fuel meets strict performance and environmental criteria.

Key EN590 Requirements
  • Sulfur Content: Maximum 10 PPM (parts per million)
  • Cetane Number: Minimum 51
  • Density: 820-845 kg/m³ at 15°C
  • Flash Point: Minimum 55°C
  • Cold Filter Plugging Point: Varies by climate

Benefits of EN590 Diesel

Engine Performance
  • • Improved combustion efficiency
  • • Enhanced power output
  • • Reduced engine wear
  • • Better cold starting
Environmental Impact
  • • Lower sulfur emissions
  • • Reduced particulate matter
  • • Better catalytic converter performance
  • • Compliance with emission standards
